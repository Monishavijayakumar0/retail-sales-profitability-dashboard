# Retail Sales & Profitability Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data to understand sales performance,
profitability, regional trends, and the impact of discounts.

The analysis uses Python, SQL, and Power BI to transform raw sales data
into meaningful business insights.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- SQL
- SQLite
- Power BI
- Jupyter Notebook

## Project Objectives
- Analyze total sales and profit.
- Compare sales and profitability across product categories.
- Identify regional sales and profit trends.
- Examine the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Key Findings
- Total sales were approximately $2.30 million.
- Total profit was approximately $286,397.
- Overall profit margin was approximately 12.47%.
- Technology generated the highest total profit among the three categories.
- The West region had the highest total sales and profit.
- Furniture had substantially lower total profit than Technology
  and Office Supplies.
